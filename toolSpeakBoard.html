Speak Board

Tap a tile to speak it aloud.

Speak Board is a free communication aid for clinic use. It is not a certified medical device, is not FDA-cleared, is not billable, and does not replace assessment by a speech-language pathologist.

Privacy: in Gaze mode, camera video is processed entirely in your browser and is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted.

Gaze mode: Cursor — look at a tile and hold until the blue bar fills to select. Calibrate once per device for best accuracy. Directional — look up / down / left / right to move a highlight, look ahead or blink to select. Head pointer — steers with head movement; the most reliable option under glare or with glasses.

Practice: a bubble-popping mini-game for training gaze and dwell selection.

Voices: uses the voices built into your device (many include male and female options — choose in Settings).

Tip: on iPhone/iPad (iOS 18+) and Android, the built-in Eye Tracking or Camera Switches accessibility features can also operate this board hands-free without Gaze mode.

Built from: Web Speech API (voices provided by your device's operating system) · MediaPipe Face Landmarker (© Google, Apache License 2.0), used in Gaze mode only · board phrases are original · tile artwork: device emoji fonts.
