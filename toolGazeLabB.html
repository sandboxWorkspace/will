Gaze Lab B is an experimental engine comparison page — not for patient use. Quality upgrades over production and Gaze Lab A:

Worker inference — the vision model runs off the UI thread, so the board never hitches while tracking. One Euro smoothing — the cursor is steady when your gaze is still and responsive when it moves. Blendshape blinks — selection by blink uses the model's own eye-closure scores. Relaxed thresholds — tracking holds on in glare and dim light. Auto mode — blends the eye cursor with a head-tilt cursor, leaning on your head whenever the iris signal gets noisy.

Calibration shows a green cursor converging onto the targets as you go — that is the live calibrated gaze. It also accepts more samples (median-filtered), so points no longer stall when your head moves naturally.

Privacy: camera video is processed entirely in your browser and is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted.

Diagnostics: enable "Show tracking readout" in Settings to see FPS, engine backend, and jitter (lighting warnings).
